# Code of Conduct

We expect project participants to communicate respectfully, focus on the
technical issue, and make room for different levels of experience.

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, deliberate disclosure of private
information, and repeated disruption are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit
or remove content and restrict participation when necessary to protect the
community.

Report conduct concerns privately to `chihao@ict.ac.cn`. Reports will be
reviewed confidentially and handled in proportion to the circumstances.
